NVIDIA GeForce NOW is a cloud gaming service that allows users to stream high-quality games on various devices without needing powerful local hardware. Utilizing NVIDIA's cloud servers, it delivers smooth gameplay with low latency and supports games from major digital stores.

My role
Senior Product Designer
Other Participants
Product Lead Designer · Product Analyst · Project Owner

Task

To create a new F.A.Q section for NVIDIA GeForce NOW to prevent issues with users who are having difficulty installing and running games or who are unable to contact support.

  • Reduce the percentage of users who discontinue using the product due to the inclusion of clear instructions on how to install the game.
  • Provide easy access to support so users can quickly resolve technical issues.
  • Enhanced user satisfaction by making the support experience more intuitive — improved Information Architecture.
  • Create a new F.A.Q structure that logically simplifies navigation and separates reference materials from technical support.

Problems

User Research & Metrics Analysis: Key Issues Identified. Based on a combination of user research and metrics analysis from over 1,200 GeForce NOW users, the following challenges were identified:

  • 41% of users complained that the «F.A.Q..» section did not help solve the problem.
  • 23,5% of users discontinued using the platform because there were no video instructions for game launching, leaving new users struggling to get started.
  • A significant volume of complaints came from users who struggled to access support or find relevant answers.
  • The F.A.Q section and support pages contained outdated information, leading to longer resolution times.
  • Outdated interfaces failed to align with users' need for quick access to information, resulting in high bounce rates and low user engagement.

My approach

  1. User Behavior Analysis
    • Used Metric to collect analytics on user interactions with the F.A.Q section, which helped identify weaknesses and key drop-off points.
    • Applied Customer Journey Mapping (CJM) to analyze the user's journey in detail, identifying frustration points and areas that needed improvement.
    • Based on this data-driven approach, I studied user behavior and search patterns, allowing me to accurately understand what users were looking for and structure the F.A.Q accordingly.
  2. Generating hypotheses and creating design concepts based on them
  3. Creating new variations in Figma, features, and user interfaces based on the core framework of NVIDIA

Hypothesis & Strategy

Core Hypothesis

“A modernized support hub with context-aware search, video guides, and intuitive IA will reduce churn by 35% and improve resolution speed.”

Strategy

  • Smart Search: Leverage user query data to prioritize results.
  • Dynamic Content: Video tutorials for high-priority pain points.

New Solution: Guides

Result

By analyzing user behavior and tracking analytics, identified the most common pain points and search patterns. This data-driven approach provided a clear understanding of what users were looking for, allowing for an optimized user journey. The old F.A.Q section was restructured into three key blocks: «Smart Search,» «Guides & Manuals,» and a «Popular Answers» section with its own subsections. This improved content navigation, enabling users to quickly find exactly what they needed — whether it was technical support or usage guides.

  • Reduced the bounce rate on the support page by 43%
  • Increased user satisfaction by 29% (based on Voice of the Customer (VoC) survey results conducted after launch)
  • Created new design variations in Figma, ensuring alignment with NVIDIA's core design framework. Implemented video tutorials, making step-by-step instructions easier for users and reducing frustration.
  • Video guides increased user engagement by 23,8%
  • For new users, the drop-off rate before completing registration decreased by 69% (based on a survey conducted after launch)
